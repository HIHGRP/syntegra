Fee clearance check

Before admitting a learner who was enrolled elsewhere, check for unpaid fees or dues at any institution in the state and, through the national clearance network, in every other state on the network. The check informs your decision — it never refuses admission and never holds back the learner's records (principle P9). Learners entering public basic education are always admitted.
